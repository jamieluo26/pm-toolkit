# Trigger and boundary examples

Use: create a durable module handover handbook; update its historical changes and current
capabilities after an evidenced release; index design decisions and known gaps.

Skip: a weekly status message; a single feature brief; execute a deployment; repair a
business application; discover personal or company files without a supplied scope.

Boundary: a local build record can be indexed but cannot prove production deployment.
An unavailable input stays unread. Conflicting rule authorities stay unresolved.
