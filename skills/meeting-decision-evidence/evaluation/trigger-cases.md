# Trigger and boundary examples

Use: compare decisions across two meetings; preserve differing outcomes for distinct
contexts; derive a cited requirements-change proposal; expose unresolved decision conflicts.

Skip: automatic task synchronization, writing a weekly report, person profiling, executing
product edits or transcribing audio.

Boundary: anonymous speaker labels remain anonymous. A later remark does not alone settle
a conflict. A partial transcript cannot establish full-record review.
